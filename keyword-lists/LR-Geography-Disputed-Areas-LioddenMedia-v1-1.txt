LR-Geography - Disputed countries, areas and names
==================================================
Version 1.1 - 2026-11-04 - Liodden Media
Applies to: LR-Geography-Countries-LioddenMedia-v1-3.txt and
            Geography Keyword Builder plugin v0.9.290

PLEASE READ BEFORE IMPORTING THE KEYWORD LIST.


1. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
-----------------------
Some countries, regions and place names on the list are disputed. The
keyword lists are a tool for finding your photos again. They are not a
political statement. This file explains:

  - the principle we follow (section 2)
  - what each dispute is about and how it is shown in the list (3 to 6)
  - how you can change any of it in your own catalogue (section 7)

Lightroom keyword files cannot contain comments: every line in the .txt
file becomes a keyword. That is why this explanation is a separate file.


2. OUR PRINCIPLE
----------------
We follow the United Nations as far as possible:

  - Countries: the 193 UN member states.
  - Borders and regions: the UN General Assembly and Security Council
    positions (for example on Crimea and Northern Cyprus).
  - Territories: listed under [TERRITORIES] with the administering
    country as a synonym, as on the UN list of non-self-governing
    territories. Exception: Western Sahara has no country synonym,
    because who should administer it is the dispute itself (section 3).

Exceptions, and why:

  - A few places that are not UN member states are still included
    because photographers travel there and need a keyword for them:
    Vatican City, Palestine, Kosovo, Taiwan, Greenland, Antarctica and
    the territory Western Sahara (section 3).
  - Where the UN has no position on a line of control (for example
    Kashmir), we follow the authority that administers the area today.
  - In a few places the plugin data does not yet follow the UN position.
    These are marked "DIFFERS FROM UN" below and are being reviewed.


3. ENTRIES THAT ARE NOT UN MEMBER STATES
----------------------------------------
Palestine
  The UN has recognised Palestine as a non-member observer state since
  2012, and most UN member states recognise it. Israel and some other
  countries do not. The list shows Palestine as a country, with the
  Gaza Strip and the West Bank under it.

Kosovo
  Kosovo declared independence from Serbia in 2008. About half of the UN
  member states recognise it. Serbia, Russia, China and others do not.
  Kosovo is not a UN member. The list shows Kosovo as a country, and the
  Serbia data does not include Kosovo, so there is no double entry.

Taiwan
  Since 1971 China's UN seat has been held by the People's Republic of
  China, which regards Taiwan as part of China. Taiwan (Republic of
  China) governs itself, and only a few states recognise it formally.
  The list shows Taiwan as its own entry because it is a separate
  travel destination with its own administration. Kinmen and Lienchiang
  (Matsu) are under Taiwan.

Greenland
  Greenland is an autonomous territory within the Kingdom of Denmark,
  not an independent country. It is listed on its own (as in the ISO
  country code list) because it is a major destination with its own
  geography. Its status has been the subject of public debate.

Vatican City
  Vatican City is not a UN member state, but it has observer status at
  the UN (formally held by the Holy See, the Pope's government of the
  Catholic Church). Its status is not disputed.

Western Sahara
  The UN lists Western Sahara as a non-self-governing territory whose
  final status has not been decided. Morocco controls most of it; the
  Polisario Front (Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic) claims it. The
  list shows Western Sahara under [AFRICA] > [TERRITORIES] with the
  synonym {Sahara Occidental}, and deliberately WITHOUT a country
  synonym. In the plugin it is ticked under Morocco ("Western Sahara -
  Africa") only because that is where the data comes from; the keyword
  itself is exported under [TERRITORIES], not under Morocco. Its towns
  (Laâyoune, Dakhla, Smara, Boujdour and others) are added below it at
  detail level 2 or higher. The Moroccan region Laâyoune-Sakia El Hamra
  only contains Tarfaya Province, which lies north of Western Sahara.
  If you imported Western Sahara regions under Morocco from an earlier
  version of the plugin, move or delete them in Lightroom.

Antarctica
  Under the Antarctic Treaty, territorial claims are frozen. The list
  includes Antarctica as one entry and does NOT include national claims
  (Norwegian, British, Argentine, Chilean, French, Australian or
  New Zealand sectors).


4. DISPUTED REGIONS INSIDE COUNTRIES
------------------------------------
These are not on the country-level list itself. They appear when you
generate more detail for a country in the plugin.

Europe
  Crimea and Sevastopol - under Ukraine. Annexed by Russia in 2014; the
    UN General Assembly (resolution 68/262) affirms Ukraine's borders.
  Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson - under Ukraine. Russia claimed
    to annex them in 2022; the UN General Assembly (ES-11/4) rejected
    this. The Russia data has 83 regions, without these areas.
  Abkhazia and South Ossetia (Tskhinvali region) - under Georgia.
    Recognised as states only by Russia and a few others.
  Transnistria - under Moldova, as "Administrative-Territorial Units of
    the Left Bank of the Dniester". Not recognised by any UN member.
  Northern Cyprus - Kyrenia and Famagusta are under Cyprus. Only Turkey
    recognises the "Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus" (UN Security
    Council resolution 541).
  Nagorno-Karabakh - under Azerbaijan. Azerbaijan took full control in
    2023, and most of the Armenian population left.
  Ceuta and Melilla - under Spain. Morocco claims them.
  Gibraltar - under the United Kingdom. Spain claims it.

Asia
  Kashmir - divided. Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh are under India; Azad
    Jammu and Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are under Pakistan. India and
    Pakistan both claim the whole region, and China controls Aksai Chin.
    We follow the line of control.
  Arunachal Pradesh - under India. China claims it as "South Tibet".
  Golan Heights - under Israel (Golan Sub-district, Northern District).
    DIFFERS FROM UN: Israel annexed the area in 1981, and the UN Security
    Council (resolution 497) declared this null and void. The UN regards
    it as occupied Syrian territory.
  East Jerusalem - under Israel (Jerusalem District).
    DIFFERS FROM UN: the UN regards East Jerusalem as occupied
    Palestinian territory (Security Council resolution 478).
  Kuril Islands (Iturup, Kunashir, Shikotan, Habomai) - under Russia.
    Japan claims the four southern islands as its "Northern Territories".
  Dokdo / Takeshima - under South Korea, which administers the islets.
    Japan claims them as Takeshima.
  Senkaku Islands - under Japan, which administers them. China and
    Taiwan claim them as Diaoyu / Diaoyutai.

Africa
  Somaliland - under Somalia. It has governed itself since 1991 but is
    not recognised by the UN or by most countries.
  Mayotte - French overseas department. Comoros claims it, and older UN
    General Assembly resolutions support Comoros.
  Scattered Islands {Îles Éparses} - French. Madagascar claims Glorioso,
    Juan de Nova, Europa and Bassas da India; Mauritius claims Tromelin.
  British Indian Ocean Territory (Chagos Islands) - listed under
    [TERRITORIES] with {United Kingdom}. The International Court of
    Justice (2019) and the UN General Assembly (resolution 73/295) said
    the islands should be returned to Mauritius. The UK and Mauritius
    agreed on a transfer of sovereignty in 2025. This entry will be
    updated when the transfer takes effect.

Americas
  Falkland Islands, South Georgia and South Sandwich Islands - under
    [TERRITORIES] with {United Kingdom}. Argentina claims them (Islas
    Malvinas). The UN lists the Falkland Islands as a non-self-governing
    territory administered by the UK. The list follows the actual
    administration and does not use the name Malvinas as a synonym.
  Essequibo - under Guyana. Venezuela claims the region.


5. DISPUTED NAMES
-----------------
  North Macedonia - name agreed with Greece in 2018 (Prespa agreement).
  Turkey {Türkiye} - the UN has used "Türkiye" since 2022.
  Ivory Coast - the official name is Côte d'Ivoire.
  Myanmar - also known as Burma.
  Eswatini - formerly Swaziland.
  Persian Gulf - Arab states use "Arabian Gulf".
  Sea of Japan - Korea uses "East Sea".
  South China Sea - name accepted, but the sea is heavily disputed
    (Spratly and Paracel Islands).

The list uses the most common English name. Add the other name as a
synonym if you prefer it (section 7).


6. SYNONYMS IN NON-LATIN SCRIPTS
--------------------------------
Country synonyms are only included when they are written in Latin
letters (accented letters such as Ísland, Việt Nam and Österreich are
included). Names in Arabic, Cyrillic, Greek, Hebrew, Chinese, Japanese,
Korean, Thai and other scripts are left out. This keeps the list
readable for most users. It is not a judgement of any language, and you
are welcome to add your own synonyms in any script.


7. HOW TO CHANGE THE STATUS OF A COUNTRY OR AREA
------------------------------------------------
Your Lightroom catalogue is yours. You can change any of this.

A) Before importing (edit the .txt file)
   Open the file in a plain text editor (TextEdit in plain-text mode,
   Notepad, BBEdit, VS Code). Each level of the hierarchy is ONE TAB.
   - Delete an entry: delete its line and all more-indented lines below.
   - Move an entry: cut its line (and the lines below it) and paste it
     under another parent, adjusting the number of TABs.
   - Add a synonym: add a line with {Name} one TAB deeper than the entry.
   Do NOT use commas or slashes in any name: Lightroom will reject the
   file. Save as UTF-8.

B) After importing (in Lightroom Classic)
   - Move: in the Keyword List panel, drag the keyword onto its new
     parent. All photos keep the keyword.
   - Rename or add synonyms: right-click the keyword > Edit Keyword Tag.
     Write synonyms in the Synonyms field.
   - Delete: right-click > Delete. Photos lose the keyword.
   - Hide from export: in Edit Keyword Tag, untick "Include on Export".

C) In the Geography Keyword Builder plugin
   - When you generate a country, untick the regions you do not want
     (for example Crimea under Ukraine). Then create them yourself in
     Lightroom under the parent you prefer.
   - Territories can be ticked or unticked individually under Remote
     Islands for the administering country. Western Sahara is found
     under Morocco.
   - Note: keywords you move in Lightroom will be created again in the
     original place if you later re-import a list from the plugin. Move
     or delete them again after importing, or edit the .txt first (A).
   - Note: GPS lookup uses the country returned by the map service
     (OpenStreetMap), which can differ from the list for disputed areas.
