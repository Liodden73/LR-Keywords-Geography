Structure:
  [GEOGRAPHY] > [WORLD] > [OCEANS AND SEAS] {Ocean} {Sea}
    Arctic Ocean / Atlantic Ocean (North + South) / Indian Ocean /
    Pacific Ocean (North + South) / Southern Ocean
      > named seas > gulfs, bays and straits

Contents: 122 keywords, 8 synonyms.
Synonyms: {Ocean}, {Sea}, {Framstredet}, {Norskehavet}, {La Manche},
{Mar Argentino}, {Sea of Cortez}, {Antarctic Ocean}

Decisions:
- Local waters (Svalbard, Falkland Islands, South Georgia, South Orkney
  Islands, Antarctic Peninsula) are NOT in this list; they are handled by
  GPS polygons (see tools/polygon-editors/).
- Politically sensitive synonyms removed ({East Sea}, {Arabian Gulf},
  {Mar de Hoces}); no Malvinas names.
- Great Australian Bight placed under Southern Ocean (Australian usage).
- Gulf of Guinea placed under North Atlantic Ocean.
- Most Norwegian synonyms removed on user's request.

Checks: no commas, no slashes, no duplicate keywords.
Not yet import-tested in Lightroom.

Development history (drafts v0-1 to v0-4) kept outside the repository.
