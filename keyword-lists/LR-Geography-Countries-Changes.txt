v1.0  (2026-11-04)  - first published version
----------------------------------------------

File: LR-Geography-Countries-LioddenMedia-v1-0.txt
Lightroom keyword import file (UTF-8, TAB indented).

Purpose: a starting point with ALL countries down to country level. More
detail (counties, municipalities, cities, nature) is generated per country
in the Geography Keyword Builder plugin and merges into the same nodes.

Structure (identical to Generator.lua output):
  [GEOGRAPHY] > [WORLD] > [CONTINENT] > Country {native name(s)}
  [GEOGRAPHY] > [WORLD] > [CONTINENT] > [TERRITORIES] > Territory {synonyms}

Contents: 199 countries, 28 territories, 183 synonyms (423 lines).
  Africa          54 countries,  5 territories
  Antarctica       1 country,    0 territories
  Asia            47 countries,  0 territories
  Europe          47 countries,  0 territories
  North America   24 countries, 13 territories
  Oceania         14 countries,  7 territories
  South America   12 countries,  3 territories

Countries: 193 UN member states + Vatican City, Palestine, Kosovo, Taiwan,
Greenland and Antarctica.

Source: plugin v0.9.287 (ListVerification.lua COUNTRIES + data/*.lua meta).
Country names, continents and native-name synonyms are taken from the
plugin; territories and their synonyms from the `territories` field.

Not included (by design): remote islands that stay under their country
(e.g. Svalbard, Faroe Islands, Puerto Rico - phase 2/3 of the territories
plan), Antarctic claims, oceans and seas (separate list).

Checks: no commas, no slashes, no duplicate keywords, correct TAB depth;
compared against Generator.lua output for all 199 countries: 0 differences.
Not yet import-tested in Lightroom.
